Transparency

How we handle it

Every submission follows an auditable lifecycle from initial receipt to final sign-off.

Open

Ticket Registered

The complaint is securely created in the database and a reference is issued. It is immediately queued for staff review.

In Progress

Active Investigation

Campus staff have acknowledged the request, assigned maintenance personnel, and work is actively underway.

Resolved

Remediation Complete

Physical repair or technical configuration is finished. The staff adds any necessary closing records.

Closed

Lifecycle Concluded

The request is officially closed. Historical events remain preserved for accountability.
